REGIONAL DATA DICTIONARY
health_region: health-region name as supplied.
year: calendar year.
reported_cases: reported measles case count for health region and year.
population: annual health-region population estimate.
annual_incidence_per_100k: reported_cases / population * 100000.
region_total_cases_2014_2023: sum of 10 yearly case counts within region (repeats across years).
region_aair_per_100k_per_year: region_total_cases_2014_2023 / sum of 10 annual regional population estimates * 100000 (repeats across years). DO NOT SUM these repeated columns.

MONTHLY DATA DICTIONARY
year, month_number, month: national reporting month.
reported_cases: national reported cases in month.
national_annual_population: national population estimate for the corresponding year, repeated every month.
monthly_cases_per_100k_annual_population: monthly case count / annual national population * 100000; NOT an annualized monthly rate.

AGE DATA DICTIONARY
year: reporting year.
age_group: lt1 (age <1), age1_lt5 (1 to <5), age5_lt15 (5 to <15), age15_lt45 (15 to <45), age45plus (>=45).
reported_cases: national case count for age and year.
age_specific_population: blank because absent from the uploaded workbook.
age_specific_incidence_per_100k: blank because population denominator absent. Do not replace blanks with zero.

ANNUAL DATA DICTIONARY
year: reporting year.
reported_cases: national case count.
population: national annual population from sum of 20 health-region populations.
annual_crude_incidence_per_100k: annual cases / national annual population * 100000.

MASTER DATA DICTIONARY
record_type: region_year / national_month / national_age_year.
year, month, health_region, age_group: applicable observation coordinates; blank when not applicable.
reported_cases: counts for that record_type only.
population, incidence_per_100k: population and corresponding incidence where denominator is supplied; blank for age records.
region_aair_per_100k_per_year: 10-year rate attached only to region records; repeated for 10 annual rows, NOT additive.

REMAINING LIMITATIONS / RECOMMENDED OPTIMIZATIONS
1. Repeated population estimates occur in 2018/2019 and 2022/2023 for all regions. Verify method of estimation and source comparability; this is NOT corrected automatically.
2. Region-specific monthly observations are NOT provided; do not infer them from national monthly counts.
3. Age-specific population estimates are NOT provided. Obtain verified year-by-age-group denominators before calculating age-specific incidence independently.
4. Acquire GIS boundaries for 20 health regions, not merely 13 administrative provinces; check name/geometry correspondence before spatial operations.
5. Check surveillance completeness, definitional changes and whether geographic reporting regions are consistent across years.
6. Note annual regional population does not encode person-level movements; do not infer individual-level risk or transmission.
